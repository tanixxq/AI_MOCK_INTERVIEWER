# 🎤 AI Mock Interviewer

An AI-powered mock interview platform that helps users prepare for technical interviews through realistic interview sessions, instant feedback, and performance analytics.

## 🚀 Overview

AI Mock Interviewer simulates real interview experiences for Software Engineering roles. Users can choose their preferred role, difficulty level, and interview topic, answer AI-generated questions, receive intelligent feedback, and track their progress over time.

The goal is to provide an affordable and accessible interview preparation platform that helps candidates improve their technical and communication skills.
